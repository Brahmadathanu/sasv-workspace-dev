## 2. Structured-record contract the adapter will consume (v1; reshape or tell me the real field names)
- **functions** (deduplicated by `identity` + `sha256` across packets 1 and 3; conflicting duplicates are an error): `identity` (`regprocedure` text), `schema`, `owner`, `acl` (aclitem text[] or null), `secdef`, `config` (text[] or null), `def` (exact `pg_get_functiondef` text — bytes preserved), `sha256`.
- **relations**: `schema`, `name`, `kind` (r/p/v/m/S), `owner`, `acl` (+ per-column ACL), `columns[]` (`name`, `type` as `format_type`, `notnull`, `default` expression, `identity`, `generated`, `collation`), `constraints[]` (`name`, `type`, `def` from `pg_get_constraintdef`), `indexes[]` (`def` from `pg_get_indexdef`), `rls` (`enabled`, `forced`, `policies[]` with `name`, `cmd`, `permissive`, `roles`, `qual`, `with_check`), `triggers[]` (`def` from `pg_get_triggerdef`, `enabled` state). Applied in dependency order (types → sequences → tables → PK/UNIQUE → FK → indexes → triggers → RLS/policies → ACLs → owners).
- **environment**: `roles[]` (`name`, attributes incl. `bypassrls`, `inherit`, `login`, role-level `config`), `memberships[]` (`role`, `member`, `admin_option`), `schemas[]` (`name`, `owner`, `acl`), `extensions[]` (`name`, `schema`, `version`), `sequences[]` (definition, owner column, ACL), `enums[]` (`schema`, `name`, `labels[]`), `settings` (`plpgsql.variable_conflict`, `search_path`, db/role overrides).
- Unsupported extensions or any dependency that cannot be satisfied are **listed and fail the run** (never silently dropped). Synthetic rows only.
