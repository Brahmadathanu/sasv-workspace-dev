# AU-5 batch-true standard conversion cost (proposal; switch default OFF)
**Change to the existing engine (minimal):** a switch table, an absorption-variance table, `costing.fn_apply_batch_true_standard(run)`, and ONE added line in `rpc_refresh_manufacturing_cop_chain` after the POH workload refresh. The overhead, prime, COP and pricing roll-ups then read the corrected SKU allocation snapshots unchanged.
**Standard per base unit** = rate × route workload per batch ÷ normal batch output (`batch_size_ref` preferred size, the approved-standard tier). Monthly volume no longer multiplies batches onto a SKU, so manufacturing frequency is separated from standard cost. Pool minus absorbed-at-standard goes to `batch_true_absorption_variance` and is not forced into SKU cost. Products with no batch size keep the current method. The scope is manufacturing labour and POH; QC and packing are unchanged.
**Rate basis (needs a user decision):**
- APPROVED_RATE (128.02 DL; POH pool/route units): Mehasamani conversion 86.69→0.54, Thampira 5 g 3,807.19→2.28. Only 29% of POH and 25% of DL is absorbed; unabsorbed variance is ₹4.73L POH and ₹5.27L DL per month. The approved rates were based on ≥1 batch per product per month, so they understate per-batch cost.
- NORMAL_VOLUME_RATE (recommended): rate = pool ÷ Σ(workload per batch × normal monthly batch equivalents). Pools are fully absorbed at standard volume (variance 0). Mehasamani 86.69→2.00, Thampira 3,807.19→8.53. The 173 held SKUs: median conversion cost falls to 19% of before. 362/634 SKUs rise by more than 5% (higher-volume products take back the cost the slow movers were carrying). The labour normal rate must be re-approved on this basis.
**Tests (local PG, run-122 inputs):** switch OFF → no change; both bases apply; conservation is exact under NORMAL_VOLUME_RATE. Files: au5_batch_true.sql, test.sh, fixture.sql, au5_*.csv.
**Live steps (approval):** 1) apply the objects plus the chain line, switch OFF, and verify there is no output change; 2) choose the rate basis (re-approve DL rate if NORMAL_VOLUME); 3) switch ON, reopen September or run for October, rerun, accept, approve.
