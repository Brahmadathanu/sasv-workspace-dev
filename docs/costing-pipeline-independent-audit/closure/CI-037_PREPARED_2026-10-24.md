- Adds nullable IK/OK columns to `costing.sku_pricing_bridge_snapshot`: scheme paid/free qty, scheme-effective selling price, net sales realization, estimated profit (₹), profit % on MRP / selling price / realization (zero-guarded), `profit_calculation_status`, plus `profit_calculation_basis`.

- Legacy `*_margin_*_before_scheme` kept; COMMENT "Legacy MRP-based cost difference — not profit".

## Cost-scope facts
- Internal loaded cost = material + labour + conversion + production/QC/stores overheads + admin + finance-admin. No marketing pool. Regional marketing is deducted once, in realization.
- Marketing pool (16 heads) holds Advertisement, Sales Promotion, Carriage Outward, Postage & Courier — so outward distribution is in marketing, not ILC.
- Materials-stores pool holds Carriage Inward (DE) and Packing & Forwarding Charges (DE). Confirm "Packing & Forwarding" is inward/production, not outward selling.
- Free units: costs are per unit (one delivered pack); the scheme step spreads paid revenue over paid+free units, so the free unit's cost is not added again. Unverified: whether the sales-volume driver (sales history) counts free units — if it excludes them, per-unit overheads are slightly overstated.
