## Additional audit-schema readback

The live Scheme audit view has 20 columns including `actor_user_id` UUID, `reason`, `approval_reference`, `old_remarks`, `new_remarks`, `old_approval_reference`, `new_approval_reference`. The client renderer uses the other fields, but its Actor fallback keys are absent from the live view. This mismatch is a **source/metadata certainty**, whereas any claim that the screen shows a blank Actor for a real history row would require a genuine permitted row/UI observation (NOT TESTED).

The proper client remedy depends on whether the intended actor presentation is a UUID, restricted pseudonym or an authorised display name. No implicit decision to expose staff identity or add a public display-name join is taken here. Issue #303 specifies a narrowly regression-tested client fix with that distinction.
