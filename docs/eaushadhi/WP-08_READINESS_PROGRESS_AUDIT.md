# WP-08 — Overall Readiness, Audit & Progress Control

**Architecture state:** YELLOW  
**Progress:** 15%

## Objective
Provide one truthful programme/operator view of what is complete and what remains for every licensed product.

## Derived readiness principle
Overall readiness must be derived, not manually toggled.

Expected high-level components:
- licensed identity/source readiness;
- Product Details preparation/verification/stage;
- Composition preparation/verification/stage;
- QC Register preparation/verification/stage;
- required document readiness;
- terminology governance readiness;
- portal progress/audit evidence.
