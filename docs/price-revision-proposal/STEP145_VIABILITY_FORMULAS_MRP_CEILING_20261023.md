# Step 145 — Regional promotional viability calculation contract and MRP multiples of 5 (23 October 2026)

Scope: two future tabs `PROMOTIONAL VIABILITY - IK` and `PROMOTIONAL VIABILITY - OK`, spreadsheet-only references from MASTER DATA, MARKETING PROMOTIONS MASTER, PROFITABILITY OBJECTIVES and DATA VALIDATION MASTER; not directly from Supabase. The database remains immutable during workbook planning.

Required output pairs: (a) exact **minimum MRP necessary** under the selected objective and current proposed promotions, and (b) **recommended MRP rounded UP to a multiple of ₹5**, not nearest. Math `=CEILING(MAX(0,Exact_Required_MRP),5)`; for an already compliant item, recommendation is the original MRP, unless presentation expressly calls for standardized ₹5 increments. A separate rounded-up candidate `=CEILING(MAX(0,Exact_Required_MRP),5)` should always be visible. Rounding alone is never proof of compliance: recompute achieved margin at rounded candidate and mark PASS/FAIL.

Financial audit basis: Approved canonical view has `mrp_ik/ok`, `gst_percent`, `ik/ok_basic_price`, `discount_percent`, `discount_amount`, `selling_price`, `contingency_percent/value`, `pricing_cost_per_sku_ik/ok`, selected scheme paid/free; representative SKU1 MRP95, GST5 -> basic90.47619; contingency2% of basic1.80952, not MRP. In active run140, selected schemes are all No Scheme (paid1/free0). At least formula sequence Basic=MRP/(1+GST/100), Discount=Basic*proposedDiscount/100, Selling=Basic-Discount is demonstrated on current master. Define post-promotion SalesRealization only after confirming exactly whether contingency is deducted before/after scheme and whether free-unit cost spread is pricing-cost based or effective gross unit realization. Pricing cost includes internal loaded cost + regional marketing expense, should not be double-counted.

Selected profitability metric is regional and single: Profit/MRP, Profit/SellingPrice, Profit/NetSalesRealization or Profit₹. Target threshold from PROFITABILITY OBJECTIVES B/C; percent targets represented as points (e.g. 7 not .07). Missing/zero denominators, missing scheme quantity mapping, negative/infeasible targets, incompatible policy and excluded regional membership must produce explicit non-viable/error status, not false numerical PASS. Exclude price-list false SKUs from respective region assessment (do not lose SKU identity).

Important: **Do not deploy unverified proposal-money formulas** while net sales realization and scheme cost accounting remain unproven. Once source-function parity is checked, calculate rounded recommendation and re-test minimum objective. New formula tabs remain pending this guard; no database changes.
